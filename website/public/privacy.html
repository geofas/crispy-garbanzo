Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Nudgy Timer is made by Georg Fasching, who is the data controller for anything described here. Questions and requests go to hello@nudgytimer.app.

The app

The Nudgy Timer app has no account and no server. The timers you create are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch and are sent only between those two devices. The app does not collect them, and does not contain advertising or third-party tracking.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app learns only whether the unlock has been bought.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, these details are stored:

  • your email address;
  • the date and time you joined;
  • which form on the page you used.

Why: to send you one email when Nudgy Timer is available on the App Store. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form.

How long: until that email has been sent, or until you ask for your address to be deleted, whichever comes first.

Who else handles it: the list is stored with Supabase, a database provider. It is not shared with anyone else, and it is never sold.

This website

This website sets no cookies. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages and protect the site. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and does not build a profile of you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@nudgytimer.app from the address you signed up with. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
