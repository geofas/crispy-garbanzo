You are running the room. The clock is running you.

The discussion is finally going somewhere. You glance at your watch, and everyone sees you do it. Or you don't glance, and the session runs ten minutes over.

This is not a discipline problem. It is a signalling problem: the time is on a screen, and your attention belongs to the people in front of you.

Nudgy Timer moves the signal from your eyes to your wrist.

How it works

Three steps, then forget about it.

  1. Pick a timer

    Choose a ready-made timer, or build your own with up to six nudges, such as ten minutes, five minutes and time's up.

  2. Start it

    Start from your iPhone or your Apple Watch. Both show the countdown, and either can end it.

  3. Feel the nudges

    Taps arrive on your Watch, your iPhone, or both. You decide which when you set up the timer.

The tap pattern

You know where you are by the count.

Each kind of nudge feels different, so you never need to look.

One tap

An earlier nudge. Time to start steering towards the close.

Two taps

The last warning. Wrap up the point you are on.

Three taps

Time's up.

Good to know

What Apple's rules mean in practice

  • The one, two and three-tap patterns play on Apple Watch when Nudgy Timer is open as the session starts, for sessions of up to one hour.
  • After an hour, or if you switch to another Watch app, each nudge arrives as a standard notification tap with the time left.
  • Put your Apple Watch in Silent Mode. Otherwise it plays a sound with each tap.
  • The app is still in development, so details may change before release.

Questions

Before you join the list.

When can I get Nudgy Timer?

Nudgy Timer is in development and is not on the App Store yet. Join the waitlist and you will get one email when it is available.

What will it cost?

Two timers are free. Everything else is a single one-time purchase, with no subscription. The price will be announced at launch.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Nudgy Timer works on an iPhone alone, on an Apple Watch with your iPhone, or on both at once. Nudges on the wrist are the most discreet.

Which devices does it need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. For wrist nudges, an Apple Watch running watchOS 10 or later.

Will anyone else notice the nudges?

With your Apple Watch in Silent Mode, a nudge is a tap on your wrist and nothing more. Without Silent Mode, Apple Watch plays a sound with each tap, so turn Silent Mode on before you start.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no server. Your timers stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

What happens to my email address?

It is used for one purpose: to tell you when Nudgy Timer is on the App Store. It is not shared or sold, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time. The privacy policy has the detail.
