Support

Nudgy Timer is still in development. This page will grow as it reaches the App Store. For anything not covered here, email hello@nudgytimer.app.

If you cannot feel a nudge on your Apple Watch

  • Open Nudgy Timer on the Watch before or as the session starts. Nudgy Timer plays each nudge itself only while the app is running on the Watch.
  • For sessions longer than one hour, or after you switch to another Watch app, each nudge arrives as a notification with the time left.
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Nudgy Timer.

If your Apple Watch makes a sound

Turn on Silent Mode on the Watch. Without it, Apple Watch plays a sound with each tap.

If you cannot feel a nudge on your iPhone

  • In iOS Settings, turn on “Play Haptics in Silent Mode”.
  • If you use a Focus, allow Nudgy Timer in it.
  • With the iPhone locked or in another app, a nudge arrives as a notification vibration.
