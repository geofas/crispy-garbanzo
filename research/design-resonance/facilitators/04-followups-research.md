# Follow-up questions — facilitators — User Research Director

Simulated session with a synthetic participant. The answers will be hypotheses to check with real facilitators, not evidence from users.

## Questions

1. **Picture next Tuesday's bereavement group with one of these eight running. Which variant, if any, would you have on your wrist or phone in that room, where exactly would each device be, and what would have to be true before you did it? If the answer is none, say so and say why.**
   Rationale: Round 1 gave preferences split by board (A3-Web and B3-Web for calm, A2 and B/B3 for the session screen) but no statement of willingness to use any single variant in front of the group; this separates liking from use.

2. **You said dark mattered more than bright, and you also said the labelled tiles on B-Session and B3-Session were the clearest account of what is coming, while your phone is face down in practice. During a session, which screen would you actually look at, how often, and which of those two qualities would you give up if you could only have one?**
   Rationale: Resolves a contradiction between the stated visibility preference (A family) and the stated comprehension preference (B, B3), and tests whether the phone session screen matters at all for this participant.

3. **You said the B-Session violet block "gets noticed" across a circle, that a neighbour "can read" the number on every watch, and that B would leave you "faintly embarrassed". For each of those three, tell me what in the image you based it on, what you are assuming about the room, the distance and the screen, and what you would need to see or try in person before you would stand by it.**
   Rationale: These are judgements about real-room visibility and social reaction that a static render cannot show; the question separates what was observed from what was inferred, without suggesting which way the answer should go.

4. **You read "Standard taps only" as meaning the one-two-three pattern is missing, and you could not try a 90-minute timer before paying. Walk me through what you would do, step by step, from the moment you press "Upgrade" and see whatever comes next: what would make you pay, what would make you close the app, and what would you do about Tuesday's session in the meantime?**
   Rationale: Round 1 listed information wanted before paying but stayed general about the decision itself; this probes the actual stopping points (no price, no trial at session length, doubt about tap patterns) and whether any of them ends the journey regardless of design direction.

## Notes for the record

- Not asked again: icon recognition, headline preference and tap-count comprehension. The Design Director's probes covered them and the answers were specific.
- Out of scope for any answer: how the taps feel, whether two can be told from three while listening, whether the screen wakes or stays lit, and any sound. The participant has already said these cannot be judged from stills; question 3 asks them to hold the same line on room visibility.
