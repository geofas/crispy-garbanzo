# Moderator guide — facilitators

Simulated session with a synthetic participant. Output is hypotheses to check with real people, not evidence from users.

## How to work through this

- Answer in writing. Think aloud: write what you notice first, then what you make of it.
- Eight variants: A, A1, A2, A3, B, B1, B2, B3. Take them one at a time, in that order, within each task.
- Compare variants only after you have seen all eight in a task.
- If you cannot judge something from a still image (how a tap feels, movement, readability on a real wrist), say so. Do not guess.
- There are no right answers.

## Warm-up

1. Describe the last session you ran: setting, group, length, structure, and whether you worked alone.
2. How did you keep track of time in it?
3. Tell me about a time when timekeeping did not go as you wanted. What did you do next?

## Task 1 — First impressions

Boards: `<V>-Brand.png`, `<V>-Web.png`. For each variant:

1. What is this? Describe it as you would to a colleague.
2. Who do you think it is made for? What tells you that?
3. Picture it in the setting where you work. How would it sit there?
4. What is unclear or missing?

After all eight: which stayed with you, which did not, and why?

## Task 2 — In-session use

Boards: `<V>-Session.png`, `<V>-Watch.png`. Imagine you are mid-session with a group. For each variant:

1. Look for two seconds, then look away. What did you take in?
2. What do you understand about what happens next, and when?
3. What do you understand about the taps: how many, and what each means? Where did you get that from?
4. What would the people in the room see or notice, if anything?
5. What can you not judge from these images?

After all eight: what differences mattered to you, if any?

## Task 3 — Timer list and upgrade

Board: `<V>-List.png`. For each variant:

1. What do you see? What would you do first?
2. How do the timers shown relate to the sessions you run?
3. Some items appear unavailable. What is going on, and how do you react?
4. Find anything that asks you to pay or upgrade. What do you expect to get, and what would you want to know before deciding?

After all eight: did any list change your view of whether this is for you?

## Task 4 — Words

All boards. For each variant:

1. Copy out the headline and any lines that stood out. What does each say to you?
2. Which words would you use yourself? Which would you never use?
3. Does anything leave you doubtful or unsure what it means?
4. How would you describe the tone of voice? How does it relate to the way you talk about your work?

After all eight: which wording was clearest, which least clear, and why?
