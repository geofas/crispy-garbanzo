# Design probes — facilitators

Simulated session. Everything below is a hypothesis to check with real people, not evidence from users. Haptics, motion and wrist glanceability cannot be judged from these stills.

## 1. Design notes (from the images only)

- **A — Quiet Signal.** Dark navy, one amber accent. Mark: six dots in a 1-2-3 triangle, top dot amber. Space Grotesk countdown. Headline "You feel the time. The room does not." Session: thin dot track, times in one string ("7:30 · 5:00 · 2:00 · end").
- **A1.** Same UI as A. New mark: arch with a dot beneath, amber-filled default icon, lowercase "nudgy" wordmark. Tap dots all amber. Mark repeated above the countdown and in the web card.
- **A2.** UI kit, not a brand board. List rows use chevrons, one lock in the section header. Session adds a "Next nudge" card with three-slot dot indicator and labelled ticks (5:00 in amber). Web: "Discreet wrist taps before your session ends.", a Watch render, and "No account and no server".
- **A3.** Warm off-white, heavier Bricolage Grotesque. Light timer list and website; dark session and Watch. Concentric rings round the countdown, "left in this session" beneath it; next nudge in a pill.
- **B — Friendly Tap.** Light lilac, violet and yellow, rounded Fredoka. Icon: yellow dot with two arcs. Voice: "Psst. Five minutes left." Session: violet countdown block, four tiles (7:30 done, 5:00 next, 2:00 2 taps, End 3 taps). Web: "A gentle tap before time runs out."
- **B1.** Tipped white "n" with marigold dot; lowercase wordmark. Bricolage. Headline "Nearly time. Only you know." Session has no colour block: dark numerals on light, dots with times, outlined End button.
- **B2.** UI kit. Figtree throughout, plain "Nudgy" in ink. Thin-weight countdown; card with next nudge, progress line and "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps". Red-tinted End. Web: "A quiet tap on your wrist before your session ends."; "Works on an iPhone alone…".
- **B3.** B's layout in petrol and apricot on grey-white. Voice described as "like a colleague leaning over". Headline "A quiet tap before time runs out."

Common to all: demo session is 15 min; free timers 5 and 15 min; "Workshop · 90 min" is locked; every running Watch shows "Standard taps only" (explained in a caption only on B-family boards); web subline names "facilitators".

## 2. Hypotheses

1. **Headline (Web, all).** "You feel the time. The room does not." (A, A1, A3) and "Only you know" (B1) will resonate more than "before time runs out" (B, B3), because they name the room rather than the deadline. "Psst." (B-Brand) will be judged too playful for support or therapy groups.
2. **Screen presence (Session).** The saturated colour blocks in B-Session and B3-Session will raise concern about a lit phone being noticed by the group; dark A-family sessions will be judged more discreet. A3's light list with dark session may read as two different apps.
3. **Reading the nudge plan (Session).** B and B3's labelled tiles and B2's "Then…" line will be understood faster than A/A1's string of times; A2's labelled ticks will sit between.
4. **Fit to session length (List, Watch, all).** The 15-minute demo and 5/15-minute free timers will not map onto 60–120-minute sessions; the participant will go straight to "Workshop · 90 min" and react to its lock. "Standard taps only" on the Watch will cause doubt in every variant.
5. **Mark (Brand).** The dot-and-arcs icon (B, B2, B3) will be read as sound or broadcast, contradicting a silent product. A's dot triangle will be read as a count only after the explanation. A1's arch and B1's tipped n will not be decoded unaided.
6. **Reassurance copy (Web).** A2 and B2's extra lines (no account; works on iPhone alone; one email) will matter more to this audience than any palette difference.

## 3. Probes

1. Looking only at this icon, with no text: what do you think this app does?
2. Your phone is on the table or your knee with this screen showing. What happens in the room?
3. Without rereading: when is the next signal after this one, and what will it be? How did you work that out?
4. Take the last session you ran. Which timer on this list would you start? Talk me through what happens.
5. The Watch says "Standard taps only". What do you take that to mean?
6. Read the headline aloud. Whose problem is it describing? Is anything on this page speaking to something you had been wondering about?
