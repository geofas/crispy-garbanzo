# Design probes — facilitators

Simulated session: hypotheses to check with real people, not evidence from users. Haptics, motion and wrist glanceability cannot be judged from stills.

## 1. Design notes (from the images only)

- **A — Quiet Signal.** Dark navy, one amber accent. Mark: six dots in a 1-2-3 triangle, top dot amber. Space Grotesk countdown. Headline "You feel the time. The room does not." Session: thin dot track, times in one string ("7:30 · 5:00 · 2:00 · end").
- **A1.** Same UI as A. New mark: arch with a dot beneath, amber-filled default icon, lowercase "nudgy" wordmark.
- **A2.** UI kit, not a brand board. List rows use chevrons, one lock in the section header. Session adds a "Next nudge" card with a three-slot dot indicator and labelled ticks (5:00 in amber). Web: "Discreet wrist taps before your session ends.", a Watch render, "No account and no server".
- **A3.** Warm off-white, heavier Bricolage Grotesque. Light timer list and website; dark session and Watch. Concentric rings round the countdown, "left in this session" beneath; next nudge in a pill.
- **B — Friendly Tap.** Light lilac, violet and yellow, rounded Fredoka. Icon: yellow dot with two arcs. Voice: "Psst. Five minutes left." Session: violet countdown block, four tiles (7:30 done, 5:00 next, 2:00 2 taps, End 3 taps). Web: "A gentle tap before time runs out."
- **B1.** Tipped white "n" with marigold dot; lowercase wordmark. Headline "Nearly time. Only you know." Session has no colour block: dark numerals on light, dots with times, outlined End button.
- **B2.** UI kit. Figtree throughout, plain "Nudgy" in ink. Thin-weight countdown; card with next nudge, progress line and "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps". Red-tinted End. Web: "A quiet tap on your wrist before your session ends."; "Works on an iPhone alone…".
- **B3.** B's layout in petrol and apricot on grey-white. Headline "A quiet tap before time runs out."

Common to all: demo session is 15 min; free timers are 5 and 15 min; "Workshop · 90 min" is locked; every running Watch shows "Standard taps only" (captioned only on B-family boards); the web subline names "facilitators".

## 2. Hypotheses

1. **Headline (Web, all).** "You feel the time. The room does not." (A, A1, A3) and "Only you know" (B1) will resonate more than "before time runs out" (B, B3). "Psst." (B-Brand) will be judged too playful for support or therapy groups.
2. **Screen presence (Session).** The saturated blocks in B-Session and B3-Session will raise concern about a lit phone being noticed by the group; dark A-family sessions will be judged more discreet.
3. **Reading the nudge plan (Session).** B and B3's labelled tiles and B2's "Then…" line will be understood faster than A/A1's string of times.
4. **Fit to session length (List, Watch, all).** The 15-minute demo and 5/15-minute free timers will not map onto 60–120-minute sessions; the participant will go to "Workshop · 90 min" and react to its lock. "Standard taps only" will cause doubt in every variant.
5. **Mark (Brand).** The dot-and-arcs icon (B, B2, B3) will be read as sound, contradicting a silent product. A's dot triangle will be read as a count only after the explanation. A1's arch and B1's tipped n will not be decoded unaided.
6. **Reassurance copy (Web).** A2 and B2's extra lines (no account; works on iPhone alone) will matter more to this audience than any palette difference.

## 3. Probes

1. Looking only at this icon, with no text: what do you think this app does?
2. Your phone is on the table or your knee with this screen showing. What happens in the room?
3. Without rereading: when is the next signal after this one, and what will it be? How did you work that out?
4. Take the last session you ran. Which timer on this list would you start? What happens next?
5. The Watch says "Standard taps only". What do you take that to mean?
6. Read the headline aloud. Whose problem is it describing? What else on the page, if anything, changes whether you would sign up?
