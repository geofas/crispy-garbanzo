# Session round 2 — teachers (participant)

I am Hannah, a synthetic persona, not a real person. Nothing here is evidence from real users; it is a set of hypotheses to check with real teachers. I cannot judge how taps feel, any movement, or how a watch reads on a real wrist from stills.

## User Research Director

**1. Watch boards only.** You are right: my "easiest" picks were phone screens I would not see in a lesson. On the wrist all eight show the same three things: a countdown, "5 min left", and (bar A2) "1 tap". None shows what comes after, or how far through I am.
- **A, A1:** time, then "5 min left · 1 tap" in small white. Tells me the next warning only.
- **A2:** dots replace the words "1 tap". Fails.
- **A3:** the same line in an outlined pill; it stands out a little more.
- **B:** yellow pill, the most visible next-warning line. Round letters.
- **B1:** as A in purple.
- **B2:** thinnest, largest numbers; the line is orange and all in words. The best of the eight.
- **B3:** as B1 in teal.
- A-family lists show a locked "Meeting · 30 min" and "Upgrade on iPhone". B-family lists show two timers and nothing else, so no sign a lesson timer exists.

**2. Someone asks what it is.**
- **B3:** a pupil sees a dark watch with a timer; "it's a timer." The speaker icon is the risk: a colleague may assume it makes noise. I would still wear it.
- **B2:** same. The least remarkable; nothing to explain.
- **A2:** same; dark and plain.
- None would stop me wearing it in the lab. The running watch screens are all dark and look alike from a metre away. Only B's yellow pill and round letters would draw a comment.

**3. What I would expect to feel in a lesson.** One tap at the earlier warnings, two at the last warning, three at the bell.
- **Could say after one look:** B-Session and B3-Session (tiles), B2-Session (the "Then…" line), A-Web and A3-Web (the list in the dark panel), every Brand board with the tap pattern box.
- **Could not say:** every Watch board (only the next tap), A-Session and A2-Session (times but no tap counts beyond the next).
- No board shows a 50-minute lesson, so where the warnings would fall is a guess.

**4. First school day, free timers only.**
- Period 1: I forget. Period 2: with 15 minutes left I start "15 min" from the Watch. That needs me to look at the clock first, which is the thing I fail to do. If I manage it, I get taps at 7:30, 5:00, 2:00 and the end, which is useful for packing away.
- By period 3 or 4 I know whether I can feel the taps apart. If yes, I want the 50-minute timer. On the A-family watch I see "Upgrade on iPhone"; the phone is in the drawer, so it waits until break. On the B-family watch nothing tells me there is more.
- At break I open a List board: padlocks, "Upgrade to access", no price. If the next screen shows a small one-off price, I pay that day. If it is more than a few pounds, or it is A2-List's arrow leading to a payment screen, I go back to the alarm.

## Design Director

**Q1.**
- The typeface does more. B1's letters look adult while still purple. B3 changes both, so I cannot credit teal alone.
- With "Psst" covered, B still reads as made for pupils: fat round letters and bright yellow.
- A3-Web stays with the manager. The light ground makes it pleasanter, not mine. What holds it there is "meeting chairs" first and "session".

**Q2.**
- Both read as a speaker. B3's faded outer arc makes it slightly less so.
- Mainly the solid orange field. The dark version with a pale arch is quieter; I would choose that one.
- Closest: the tipped n with a dot, as a nudge at the shoulder. Furthest: the blob with arcs. The dot triangle makes sense only after the caption. None says "wrist" unaided.

**Q3.**
- B2 is faster. Mainly because it tells me what follows; darker-on-light text helps a little.
- Tiles hold on both. B3's are calmer; B's yellow tile is found fractionally sooner.
- Colour first, then size. B3's teal block is as big but would not catch an eye across a bench.
- Tiles, or words with tap counts. On the phone, light is fine; it is in a drawer.

**Q4.**
- Needs, in order: time left; next warning and its tap count; what follows; a start I cannot trigger by accident; lesson name last.
- **A:** extra "Standard taps only"; missing what follows.
- **A2:** extra dots; missing the tap count in words.
- **B2:** extra "Standard taps only"; missing what follows.
- **B3:** extra, a large "End session" button for a sleeve to catch; missing what follows.
- The dots: no words beside them, and they sit where page dots sit. "1 tap" beside them would do; then the dots are spare, so remove them.
- I would miss nothing. Same answer for both families.
