# Design read-out — teachers (simulated session)

One synthetic persona ("Hannah", secondary science teacher), no real users. Everything below is a hypothesis to check with real teachers. Haptics, motion and wrist glanceability were not judged.

## 1. Hypotheses: held or not

- **H1 — held.** B: "I took it for something aimed at pupils or a primary classroom." B2-Web: "plain and adult". With "Psst" covered, B "still reads as made for pupils: fat round letters and bright yellow."
- **H2 — partly held.** A-Session "would draw little attention"; B-Session is "a big purple block; anyone near my desk would see it"; the tiles gave "the whole sequence in one look." Not held for B3: its block "would not catch an eye across a bench."
- **H3 — held.** A2-Web is "the first where I knew what it was at once"; from A-Web she "could only say 'something about feeling time'." Her words: "tap, vibrate, wrist, lesson".
- **H4 — held.** "The one timer named for my job is locked." A2-List: "I would expect 'Lesson · 50 min' to open, and feel caught out by a payment screen."
- **H5 — held.** "'Standard taps only' confuses me on every one." A2-Watch: "I would not work out that one filled dot of three means one tap."
- **H6 — held.** A: the triangle "meant nothing until I read the caption." A1: "a doorway or a magnet." B1: "a letter n with a dot, nothing more." Prompted, the tipped n came closest, but "none says 'wrist' unaided."

## 2. What drove resonance

- **Literal headlines.** A2-Web, B2-Web. B2's is "the one I would repeat."
- **Whole sequence with tap counts.** B-Session and B3-Session tiles; B2-Session's "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps". B2 beat A2 "mainly because it tells me what follows", not because of the ground.
- **Adult typeface.** B1 "looks adult while still purple".
- **Calm colour.** B3 scored the only 5 for appeal.
- **Worded Watch line.** B2-Watch: "the line is orange and all in words. The best of the eight."

## 3. What blocked it

- **Fredoka and saturated yellow.** B-Brand, B-Web; B-List is "the most toy-like".
- **Riddle headline.** A-Web, A1-Web, A3-Web: "read it twice. Sounds like an advert."
- **Audience framing.** "Meeting chairs" first and "session" keep the A family "with the manager"; A3's light ground did not change that.
- **Icons.** The B and B3 blob reads as "a speaker, which suggests sound." A1's amber field is "the loudest icon on my phone".
- **Watch.** No board shows what follows the next tap. A-family lists say "Upgrade on iPhone" while the phone is in a drawer; B-family lists give "no sign a lesson timer exists."
- **List.** No price on any board; A2-List chevrons on locked rows.
- **Decoration.** A3-Session rings: "a target or a breathing app."

## 4. Keep / change

**Keep**
- B2-Web and A2-Web headlines.
- B-Session and B3-Session tile row; B2-Session "Then…" line.
- B2-Watch worded line and numerals.
- B3 palette; B1 and B2 typefaces.
- A1's dark-field icon variant.

**Change**
- All Watch running screens: remove "Standard taps only"; add what follows the next tap.
- A2-Watch: replace dots with "1 tap".
- A2-List: padlocks, not chevrons.
- All List boards: show the price; test a free trial of the 50-minute timer.
- B-family Watch lists: signal that more timers exist.
- B2-Session: enlarge the "Then…" line. A-Session, A2-Session: add tap counts for every nudge.
- A3-Session: remove the rings.
- B and B3 icon: redraw the arcs.
- All Web boards: test "lesson" and the audience order.
- A2-Web: drop "no server".

## 5. Best of the A family and best of the B family for this audience

- **A family: A2.** Clearest website (comprehension 5); weakened by Watch dots and List chevrons, both fixable.
- **B family: B2,** ranked first overall; B3 second with the highest appeal. B2's structure in B3's palette is worth testing; it was not shown.
- **Overall: B family, with a caveat.** B2 and B3 lead, but original B ranked last. The preference follows literal copy, a stated sequence and adult type, not the family.

## 6. What I would not conclude from this

- One synthetic persona is not a sample. Scores and ranking show direction only.
- Her context (50-minute periods, phone in a drawer, Watch-only start) is unverified desk research. If wrong, the weight on the Watch boards changes.
- Whether taps can be told apart while moving, whether a watch on a bench is audible, and how a screen reads on a wrist cannot be answered here.
- B3's lead may be typeface, colour or both; she "cannot credit teal alone."
- The renders approximate a design canvas; fine legibility judgements, such as B1's "faint" dots, may be artefacts.
