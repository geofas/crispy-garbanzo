# Moderator guide — teachers

Simulated design-testing session. No real users are involved; answers are hypotheses to check with real teachers.

## How to work through this

- Answer in writing, as the teacher you are.
- Think aloud: write what you notice first, then what you make of it.
- Judge only what is visible. If you cannot judge something from a still image (how a tap feels, movement, reading the watch on a real wrist), say so and move on.
- No right answers; indifference and confusion are useful.
- Order: A, A1, A2, A3, B, B1, B2, B3. Comment on each on its own. Compare variants only in the final question of each task.

## Warm-up (before opening any board)

1. Describe a typical session you run: length, setting, who is in the room, how it ends.
2. How do you keep track of time during it today?
3. Describe the last time the timing of a session did not go as you intended.

## Task 1 — first impressions

Boards: `<V>-Brand.png` and `<V>-Web.png`, per variant.

1. What is this? Answer before reading closely.
2. Who do you think it is made for? What tells you that?
3. Picture this on your phone, watch or screen where you teach. How would it sit there?

After all eight: which variants stayed with you, which did not, and why?

## Task 2 — in-session use

Boards: `<V>-Session.png` and `<V>-Watch.png`, per variant. Imagine you are mid-session.

1. Look for about two seconds, then look away. What did you take in?
2. What, if anything, would people in the room notice?
3. What do you understand about what happens next, and when?
4. What do you understand about the taps: how many, and what each means? What told you?

After all eight: where was the session state easiest and hardest to read, and why?

## Task 3 — timer list and upgrade

Board: `<V>-List.png`, per variant.

1. What do you see? What would you tap first?
2. How do the timers shown relate to the sessions you run?
3. Some items may appear unavailable. What do you think is going on, and how do you react?
4. Find anything that asks you to pay or upgrade. What do you expect to get, and what would you want to know before deciding?

After all eight: how did the lists differ for you, if at all?

## Task 4 — words

All boards, per variant.

1. Copy out the headlines and short lines you noticed.
2. What does each say to you?
3. Which words would you use yourself when describing this to a colleague? Which would you not?
4. Did any line make a claim you would want explained or proven?

After all eight: which lines do you remember without looking back?

## Close

1. What would have to be true for you to use it? What would stop you?
