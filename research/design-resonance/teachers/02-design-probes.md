# Design probes — teachers

Simulated session, no real users: everything below is a hypothesis to check with real teachers. Haptics, motion and wrist glanceability cannot be judged from static boards.

## 1. Design notes

**A — Quiet Signal.** Near-black navy, one amber accent. Mark is a triangle of six dots, top dot amber. Headline "You feel the time. The room does not." Session: large white countdown, thin four-point progress line, coral "End session".

**A1 — arch and dot.** Same UI and copy as A. Mark is an arch with an amber dot beneath; default icon is a full amber field.

**A2 — native UI kit.** A's palette as system-style controls. Locked rows show chevrons, not locks. Session adds a "Next nudge" card and a ticked progress bar. Literal web headline: "Discreet wrist taps before your session ends", plus "No account and no server". Watch running screen shows dots and "5 min left", no worded tap count.

**A3 — warmed, light mode.** Heavier Bricolage Grotesque, warm off-white. List and website are light; session and Watch stay dark. Concentric rings around the countdown.

**B — Friendly Tap.** Pale lilac ground, saturated violet and yellow, rounded Fredoka type. Icon is a yellow blob with two arcs. Voice: "Psst. Five minutes left." Session has a violet countdown block and four nudge tiles (done, next, 2 taps, 3 taps).

**B1 — tipped n.** Deeper violet with marigold; Bricolage Grotesque. Icon is a tilted white n with a dot. Headline "Nearly time. Only you know." Session: dark countdown on pale ground, a row of dots.

**B2 — friendly, grown-up.** Plain sans, standard inset lists. Violet controls, honey for the next nudge only, red-tinted "End session". Session card states what follows: "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps". Web: "A quiet tap on your wrist before your session ends."

**B3 — petrol and apricot.** B's layout in dark teal and apricot on off-white. Headline "A quiet tap before time runs out."

**Common to all.** Free timers are 5 and 15 min; "Lesson · 50 min" is locked. Every website names "meeting chairs, facilitators, trainers and teachers", in that order. Every Watch running screen carries "Standard taps only". A-family Watch lists show a locked "Upgrade on iPhone" row; B-family lists do not.

## 2. Hypotheses

- **H1 — B Brand, B Web.** Rounded type, bright violet/yellow and "Psst" will read as made for pupils or primary classrooms, not for a secondary teacher's own use. B2 and B3 will not.
- **H2 — A, A2, B, B3 Session.** Dark A-family screens will be judged less noticeable to a class than B's and B3's colour blocks. Their four-tile row will still explain what comes next most clearly.
- **H3 — A Web vs A2 Web, B2 Web.** The literal headlines will be understood faster than "You feel the time. The room does not." Participants will restate the product with "vibrate", "tap" and "lesson", not "haptic", "nudge" or "session".
- **H4 — all List boards.** A locked "Lesson · 50 min" beside free 5 and 15 min timers will be read as withholding the one timer a teacher needs. A2's chevrons will be read as available, not locked.
- **H5 — all Watch boards.** "Standard taps only" will not be understood. A2 Watch will convey the tap count less reliably than variants that write "1 tap".
- **H6 — A, A1, B1 Brand.** A's dot triangle will be linked to the tap pattern only after reading the caption; A1's arch and B1's tipped n will not be read as a wrist or a person unprompted.

## 3. Probes

1. Looking at this board alone: whose phone would you expect to find this on? What makes you say that?
2. Your phone is on your desk showing this screen. What does a pupil in the front row see?
3. Without looking back: what happens next in this session, and how will you know it has happened?
4. Read the headline once, then tell me in your own words what this does, as you would to a colleague.
5. Which timer on this list would you use for your next lesson? Talk me through what happens when you try.
6. Point to anything on this Watch screen, or in the app icon, whose meaning you are unsure of. What is your best guess?
