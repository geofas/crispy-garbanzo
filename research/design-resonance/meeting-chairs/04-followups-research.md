# Session round 2 — meeting chairs — User Research Director follow-ups

Simulated session with a synthetic participant. Answers are hypotheses to check with real people, not evidence from users.

## Follow-up questions

1. **You ranked B2 first and B3 third, and you also said B2's "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps" line is too small for a glance while B3's tiles gave you "the whole plan without thinking". Walk me through how you weighed those two things when you placed them.**
   Rationale: the ranking contradicts the in-session finding; this separates what she can read mid-session from what she is comfortable having on the table.

2. **Think of your next session, with the organiser's cards on the table as before. Describe, step by step, what you would do with this app on that day, if anything — and what you would do with the cards and the stopwatch.**
   Rationale: "near enough to try once" and "acceptable, if I am told the cost" are polite and non-committal; this tests willingness to use it in front of her room rather than liking, without suggesting an answer.

3. **You described several screens as "comfortable" or "discreet" for your table, and B as not. Which parts of that judgement come from what you can see in the image, and which parts depend on things the image does not show?**
   Rationale: she judged room visibility and discretion from static renders; this asks her to separate observation from assumption (screen brightness, viewing angle, whether the phone needs to be face up at all).

4. **You remembered "You feel the time. The room does not." without looking back, but ranked the variants carrying it fourth, fifth and seventh, and you did not remember the wording of the two you ranked highest. What do you make of that?**
   Rationale: memorability and preference point in opposite directions; this probes whether the headline or the explanatory copy carries more weight in her decision, without proposing which.
