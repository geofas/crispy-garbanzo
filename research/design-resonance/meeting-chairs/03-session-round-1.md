# Session round 1 — meeting chairs — participant response

## Persona (synthetic)

I am a synthetic persona, not a real person. Nothing below is evidence from users; these are hypotheses to check with real people.

- **Name:** Helen.
- **Role:** senior lecturer; I chair about three conference sessions a year (frequency adopted from the profile's hypothesis).
- **Sessions:** in person, 90 minutes, four speakers, 20-minute slots (15 talk, 5 questions). The organiser's guidelines tell me to show cards at 5 minutes, 1 minute and time up.
- **Devices:** iPhone; Apple Watch worn daily.
- **Timekeeping today:** phone stopwatch on the table beside the cards, restarted for each speaker.

## Warm-up

1. A parallel session: four speakers, about forty in the room, me at a side table at the front. No timekeeper, no stage clock.
2. Phone stopwatch, face up. I looked down every minute or so.
3. I forgot to restart the stopwatch while introducing the third speaker. I guessed, showed the 5-minute card late, she ran over, and the last speaker lost most of his questions.

## Task 1 — First impressions (Brand, Web)

- **A:** a silent timer that taps me. Dark, serious, a tool. I understood the six-dot triangle only after reading "the mark is the tap pattern itself". On A-Web the small card explained it, not the headline.
- **A1:** the same with another logo. The arch reads as a door or magnet, not an n. The solid amber icon is loud.
- **A2:** the plainest. A2-Web shows a watch and "What you feel". The first where I knew what it was before the small print. A2-Brand is a parts list; not for me.
- **A3:** A with a pale website and heavier, slightly shouty type. No new information.
- **B:** bright purple and yellow, rounded letters. Looks made for primary teachers or a habit app. "Psst." is not how I want to be addressed.
- **B1:** calmer purple, tilted n. A general consumer app. Fine.
- **B2:** light, tidy, ordinary iPhone look. B2-Web says "Works on an iPhone alone…", which no other board told me.
- **B3:** blue-green and peach. B's layout, but I would not be embarrassed by it.

Made for: all say "meeting chairs, facilitators, trainers and teachers". I am a "session chair"; I half recognise myself.

Missing everywhere: the price; whether I can set 5 and 1; whether it works with the watch screen off; what happens between speakers.

Stayed in mind: A2-Web, B2-Web, B3, and B for the wrong reason. Did not: A1, A3, B1.

## Task 2 — In-session (Session, Watch)

Two-second look:

- **A / A1-Session:** 06:12, amber dot, "Next nudge: 5 min left · 1 tap". I ignored the dotted line; its labels "7:30 · 5:00 · 2:00 · end" are bunched at the right, not under the dots.
- **A2-Session:** 06:12 and a boxed "5 min left · 1 tap". Each time sits under its mark, 5:00 in amber. Clearest of the dark ones. The three circles (one filled) I first read as "step 1 of 3".
- **A3-Session:** 06:12 in rings, "left in this session". The rings look like a breathing exercise.
- **B / B3-Session:** 05:12 on a coloured block, then tiles: "7:30 done", "5:00 next", "2:00 2 taps", "End 3 taps". The whole plan without thinking. B's block is very purple.
- **B1-Session:** 05:12, a pill, dots with times beneath. Understandable, quieter.
- **B2-Session:** 05:12, "Next nudge 5 min left · 1 tap", then "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps" — right content, too small for a glance.

Where the session stands and the next nudge: clear on all eight. What 2 and 3 taps mean: shown on the session screen only in the B family; on A, A1, A3 I would have to remember it.

Watch boards: the time and "5 min left · 1 tap" read fine as pictures. A2-Watch replaces "1 tap" with the circles, which I trust less. "Standard taps only" confuses me (probe 6).

The room: a phone on my table showing B-Session's purple block could catch the front row's eye; a dark screen less so.

Cannot judge: how the taps feel, whether I could tell two from three while speaking, readability on a real wrist, whether it keeps running with the screen off.

## Task 3 — Timer list and upgrade (List)

Same content on all eight: two free timers, six locked rows, an Upgrade button.

- First I read "15 min · nudges at 7:30, 5:00, 2:00, end". My cards are 5, 1 and 0. The 7:30 tap is no use and 2:00 is not 1:00. Near enough to try once.
- The loop button "makes it restart by itself". That might suit four speakers in a row, but my slots are 20 minutes with a changeover. Unclear.
- "Meeting", "Lesson", "Workshop", "Pomodoro" are not my sessions. The only row I want is "Your own timers · Any length, your own nudges". So the free version is a trial. Acceptable, if I am told the cost.
- Upgrade: I expect a price and a payment sheet. Beforehand I want the price, "one-time" stated here (only the websites say it), and confirmation that my own timer can do 5, 1, 0.

Differences: A2-List swaps padlocks for arrows with one small padlock in the heading; I would press a row expecting it to open. A3-List's black Upgrade button dominates. B-List and B3-List put locked rows and button in one tinted box, which says "the paid part" most plainly. The B family writes "Nudges at 5 min left and at the end", easier than "5 min, end".

## Task 4 — Words

- "You feel the time. The room does not." (A, A1, A3) — I know and they do not; how is unsaid.
- "Discreet wrist taps before your session ends." (A2) — says what it does.
- "A gentle tap…" (B) / "A quiet tap before time runs out." (B3) — fine; "quiet" beats "gentle".
- "Nearly time. Only you know." (B1) — clever, vague.
- "A quiet tap on your wrist before your session ends." (B2) — clearest.
- "Keep your eyes on the room, not the clock." — my actual problem.

I would question: "nudge" (I say "warning" or "the five-minute card"); "haptic"; "session" (to me that is the whole 90 minutes; I time a slot or a talk); "earlier nudges" (which?); "Standard taps only".

Remembered without looking: "You feel the time. The room does not." and "eyes on the room, not the clock". Not A2's or B2's wording, though they told me more.

## Close

1. **B2** — plain, schedule in words, says iPhone alone works.
2. **A2** — clearest website, dark screen; circles and arrows cost it first place.
3. **B3** — tiles explain best; sober colour.
4. **A** — discreet; dotted line needs explaining.
5. **A3** — same, plus rings I do not need.
6. **B1** — forgettable.
7. **A1** — amber icon adds nothing.
8. **B** — not on a table in front of colleagues.

Before deciding: one real session with 5/1/0 set; proof it taps with the screen off; the price.

Not asked: what happens between speakers or when one starts late; whether a tap could be mistaken for a message arriving.

## Design Director's probes

1. All eight: one tap when 5 minutes are left. A, A1, A3 say only that. A2 also marks 5:00 on the bar. B, B3 ("5:00 next" tile), B1 (dot row) and B2 ("Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps") also show what follows.
2. Comfortable: A, A1, A2, A3, B1, B2. Slightly uneasy: B3 (large block, sober colour). Not comfortable: B.
3. A/A1/A3: I will know the time and nobody else will — how is unsaid. A2: taps on my wrist before the end. B/B3: a tap before time is up — where is unsaid. B1: I am told privately it is nearly time. B2: a tap on my wrist before the session ends.
4. Content: A, A2, A3, B2, B3. Rather not: B (toy-like); A1's amber icon is brighter than I would choose. B1: indifferent.
5. "15 min": free and nearest to a talk. I want "Your own timers", which is locked.
6. "15 min": the timer I started. "06:12"/"05:12": time left. "5 min left · 1 tap": one tap at five minutes. "Standard taps only": I do not know. It seems to contradict "1 tap" — will I feel one, two, three, or an ordinary buzz? The B boards carry a caption outside the watch ("shows when distinct patterns are off"); the watch does not say that, nor why they would be off. "End"/"End session": stops it.
