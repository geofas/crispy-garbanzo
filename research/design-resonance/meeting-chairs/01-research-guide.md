# Moderator guide — meeting chairs

Simulated session with a synthetic participant. Answers are hypotheses to check with real people, not evidence from users.

## How to work through this

- Answer in writing, in the first person, and think aloud: write what you notice first, then what you conclude.
- Work through one variant at a time, in this order: A, A1, A2, A3, B, B1, B2, B3.
- Do not compare variants until you have seen all eight. Comparison questions come at the end of each task.
- If you cannot judge something from a still image (tap feel, motion, how it reads on a real wrist), say so. Do not guess.
- If something is unclear or unreadable, say that too. There are no right answers.

## Warm-up

1. Describe the last session you chaired: setting, length, number of speakers or agenda items, who was in the room.
2. How did you keep track of time in that session? What did you look at, hold or rely on?
3. Describe a moment when timekeeping went differently from what you planned. What happened next?

## Task 1 — First impressions

Boards: `<V>-Brand.png`, `<V>-Web.png`. For each variant:

1. What is this? Describe it in your own words.
2. Who do you think it is made for? What makes you say that?
3. Picture this on your phone or wrist in the setting where you chair. How would it sit there?
4. What, if anything, is unclear or missing?

After all eight: which variants stayed in your mind, and why? Which did not?

## Task 2 — In-session use

Boards: `<V>-Session.png`, `<V>-Watch.png`. Imagine you are mid-session. For each variant:

1. Look for two seconds, then look away. What did you take in?
2. What do you understand about where the session stands?
3. What do you understand about the next nudge: when it comes and what it will be?
4. What do you understand about the tap patterns and what each one means?
5. What would the people in the room see or notice, if anything?
6. What can you not judge from this image?

After all eight: how do the variants differ for you during a session?

## Task 3 — Timer list and upgrade

Board: `<V>-List.png`. For each variant:

1. What do you see on this screen? What would you do first?
2. How do the timers shown relate to the sessions you run?
3. Some items appear locked. What do you make of that?
4. What do you expect to happen if you press the upgrade button? What would you want to know before pressing it?

After all eight: how do the variants differ in how they handle the locked items and the upgrade?

## Task 4 — Words

All boards. For each variant:

1. Copy out the headline and any other lines that caught your attention.
2. What is each line telling you? Put it in your own words.
3. How does the wording compare with how you and your colleagues talk about timekeeping?
4. Which words would you question, and why?

After all eight: which lines do you remember without looking back? What do you remember them saying?

## Close

1. Having seen all eight, rank them for your own use and give one reason per position.
2. What would you need to see or try before deciding whether to use this at all?
3. What did we not ask that we should have?
