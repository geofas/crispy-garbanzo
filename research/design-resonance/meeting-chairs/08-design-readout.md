# Design read-out — meeting chairs (simulated session)

Simulated session with one synthetic participant. Nothing here is evidence from real users; every point is a hypothesis to check with real people. Haptics, motion and wrist glanceability could not be judged.

## 1. Hypotheses: held or not

1. **A2-Session and B2-Session read fastest; A and B1 need the dots explained — partly held.** A2: "Clearest of the dark ones." A-Session: "I ignored the dotted line." But B2's "Then…" line was "right content, too small for a glance", B1's dot row was "understandable", and the fastest read was the tiles on B/B3-Session: "The whole plan without thinking."
2. **B too playful; B2 and B3 accepted — held.** B: "Looks made for primary teachers or a habit app. 'Psst.' is not how I want to be addressed." B3: "I would not be embarrassed by it." B2 ranked first.
3. **Dark screen preferred on the table; B's violet block draws attention — not held as stated.** "'Dark is safer' was too broad; 'no large bright block' is nearer." B1 and B2, both light, were rated comfortable. The second half held for B-Session.
4. **Literal headlines explain faster than A-Web — held.** A2-Web: "The first where I knew what it was before the small print." A-Web: "the small card explained it, not the headline."
5. **Locked "Meeting" templates do not match; "Your own timers" is the row that matters — held.** "The only row I want is 'Your own timers'." "My cards are 5, 1 and 0."
6. **"Standard taps only" causes doubt — held.** "I do not know. It seems to contradict '1 tap'."

## 2. What drove resonance

- **Literal web copy.** A2-Web (Watch render, "What you feel") and B2-Web ("A quiet tap on your wrist…", "Works on an iPhone alone…") both scored 5 for comprehension and 4 for waitlist; every other variant scored 1–3 for waitlist.
- **Ordinary iPhone appearance.** B2-List and B2-Brand: "nothing in it would draw comment."
- **Schedule in words with tap counts.** Tiles on B-Session and B3-Session scored 5 for in-session clarity. The B family is the only one that shows what 2 and 3 taps mean on the session screen.
- **Times placed under their marks.** A2-Session's labelled bar, with 5:00 in amber.
- **Sober colour.** B3's petrol and apricot made B's layout acceptable; the dot-and-arcs mark was not an issue.
- **Paid area clearly bounded.** B-List and B3-List's tinted box; B-family wording "Nudges at 5 min left and at the end".
- **Memorable line.** "You feel the time. The room does not." (A, A1, A3) and "Keep your eyes on the room, not the clock" were recalled unprompted.

## 3. What blocked it

- **B-Brand, B-Web, B-Session:** Fredoka letter shapes first, the violet-yellow pair second, "Psst." third. Fits setting 1, waitlist 1.
- **B3-Session:** the size of the coloured block only.
- **B2-Session:** the "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps" line is too small.
- **A-, A1-, A3-Session:** timeline labels bunched at the right, not under the dots; no meaning given for 2 and 3 taps.
- **A3-Session and A3-Web:** rings read as "a breathing exercise"; heavy, tight headline read as "shouty". The light ground was fine.
- **A1-Brand:** amber icon field too bright; the arch did not read as an n.
- **A2-Watch:** three circles replacing "1 tap" — "tiny and unexplained". **A2-List:** arrows on locked rows imply the row opens.
- **Every Watch board:** "Standard taps only".
- **Every List board:** no price, no "one-time"; presets (7:30/5:00/2:00) do not match 5/1/0; loop behaviour unclear. No variant scored above 3 on "for me".
- **All boards:** "session" and "nudge" are not her words; "session chair" is missing from the audience line.

## 4. Keep / change

**Keep**
- A2-Web and B2-Web headlines and the "iPhone alone" line.
- B3-Session tile content (done / next / tap counts).
- A2-Session labelled bar.
- B2-List standard layout; B-family nudge wording.

**Change**
- All Watch boards: remove "Standard taps only" or state on the Watch what will be felt.
- A2-Watch: write "1 tap" in words; circles only alongside words.
- B2-Session: enlarge the "Then…" line or replace it with B3's tiles.
- B3-Session: reduce the countdown block.
- A-, A1-, A3-Session: align labels under the dots; add tap counts.
- A2-List: restore padlocks on locked rows.
- All List boards: show the price and "one-time" at the Upgrade button; state that own timers allow any nudge points.
- A2-Web and B2-Web: test "You feel the time. The room does not." above the literal line.
- Drop for this audience: "Psst.", Fredoka (B), A3's rings, A1's amber icon field.

## 5. Best of the A family and best of the B family for this audience, and A family versus B family overall

- **Best of A: A2** (ranked 2nd; comprehension 5, fits setting 5).
- **Best of B: B2** (ranked 1st; comprehension 5, appeal 4), with B3 supplying the stronger session screen.
- **A versus B:** no family won. The two product-led variants took the first two places; B took 1st, 3rd, 5th and 8th, A took 2nd, 4th, 6th and 7th. The difference that mattered was plain, literal, native treatment versus art direction, not dark versus light. The A family is safer on the table (fits setting 4–5 throughout); the B family explains the schedule better.

## 6. What I would not conclude from this

- One synthetic persona, an academic conference chair with 5/1/0 cards. Not a ranking for meeting chairs in general, and not evidence of demand.
- Scores are single ordinal judgements; differences of one point carry no weight.
- Discretion was judged from static images. The participant said it rests on assumptions about brightness, viewing angle and whether the phone lies face up; if taps work with the screen off, "only the watch face matters".
- Nothing on whether one, two and three taps can be told apart, on wrist readability, or on mistaking a tap for a notification.
- B2 came first on the whole package, not on its session screen; the order of B2, A2 and B3 depends on whether she looks at the phone during a session, which is unknown.
- Low "for me" scores reflect shared content (presets, audience line), so they do not separate the design directions.
- Willingness to pay was not tested; price was absent from every board.
