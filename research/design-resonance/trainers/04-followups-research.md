# Follow-up questions — trainers (User Research Director)

Simulated session. No real users are involved. The participant is a synthetic persona; her answers are hypotheses to check with real trainers.

## Questions

1. **You said you would be comfortable using all four A boards, B2 and B3 in front of a group. Take your next 40-minute talk: which one, if any, would you set running, where would the phone and the watch be, and when would you look at either of them?**
   Rationale: "comfortable with" covers six variants and does not separate liking from actual use; it also tests whether the session screen matters at all for someone whose stated problem is that the phone on the lectern "looks as if I am checking messages".

2. **You called the tiles on B-Session "the clearest account of a whole session on any board", and you also said you would not use B in front of a group. If you had to run a session with B as it stands, what would you do?**
   Rationale: contradiction between best-understood layout and rejected variant; establishes whether colour is a hard barrier or a preference, without suggesting B3 as the answer.

3. **You said A3-List is "easier to read in a bright room than A-List", that the room "would see a dark screen with digits", and that a front row would see B's purple and yellow. What on the boards are those statements based on, and which parts of them are you unsure of?**
   Rationale: these are judgements about real-room legibility and visibility at a distance, which a static render cannot show; the question lets her separate what she saw from what she inferred.

4. **You said none of the free timers fits a 40-minute talk, that "the purchase is the product" for you, and that you would not press "Upgrade" blind. You have just installed the app and are looking at the list: describe what you do next, step by step, until you either stop or have a timer you would use.**
   Rationale: the upgrade answers were stated as attitudes, not actions; this asks for the behaviour at the paywall and where she drops out, without naming a price or a remedy.

## Not asked, noted for the record

- "Standard taps only": she could not explain it and it made her doubt that two and three taps work on the Watch. Already clear enough as a hypothesis; no follow-up needed.
- The taps themselves, the Watch lighting up at a nudge and wrist glanceability: she correctly said these cannot be judged from stills. No follow-up can resolve them in this format.
