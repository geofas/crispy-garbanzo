# Design read-out — trainers (simulated session)

One synthetic persona ("Helen"); no real users. Everything below is a hypothesis to check with real trainers.

## 1. Hypotheses: held or not

- **H1 (dark reads as least noticeable; B-Session as visible) — held in part.** Lectern order: "A, B3, B2, B1, B". The cause is hue, not darkness: "B needs a different colour, not a smaller block"; "Dark is what I am used to, not a requirement."
- **H2 ("Psst" and Fredoka read as casual or for children's teachers) — held for copy, not typeface.** B is "someone addressing a primary teacher". Cause: "(c) the colours change it most; (a) the wording is second. (b) I would not notice by itself."
- **H3 (labelled ticks or a "then…" line beat unlabelled dots) — held.** "The written counts matter more than the format." B1-Session: "I count dots."
- **H4 (free tier irrelevant; padlock versus chevron makes no difference) — first half held, second not held.** "For me the purchase is the product." On A2-List: "Padlocks are more honest."
- **H5 (A headline remembered, not understood; A2 understood, forgotten) — comprehension held, memory not held.** A: "Understood only after the sub-line; sounds like an advert." A2-Web's headline "stayed with me"; A's did not.
- **H6 ("Standard taps only" raises doubt) — held.** "It makes me doubt two and three taps work on the Watch"; "the same on all five".

## 2. What drove resonance

- **Later nudges and tap counts written out:** B2-Session's "Then 2:00 left · 2 taps, end · 3 taps" (her single keep), A2-Session's labelled ticks, the tiles on B-Session and B3-Session.
- **Descriptive headlines** on A2-Web and B2-Web; A2-Web also shows the Watch, "a description of a thing, not a slogan".
- **Platform and privacy facts:** B2-Web "Works on an iPhone alone…"; A2-Web "No account and no server".
- **A labelled countdown:** A3-Session "left in this session".
- **Next nudge in a pill:** A3-Session, A3-Watch, B-Watch, "which I find first".
- **Native list:** B2-List, "cleanest list".

## 3. What blocked it

- **Purple and yellow** on B-Brand, B-Web, B-Session: "classroom app". A preference about being seen, not a barrier to use.
- **Voice:** "Psst" (B); "Nearly time. Only you know." (B1-Web, "a secret, not a timer"); "You feel the time. The room does not." (A-Web, A3-Web).
- **Session screens that need working out:** A-Session and A3-Session labels "bunched at the right", with no marking of nudges already gone; B1-Session dots without tap counts.
- **Symbols without words:** circles on A2-Watch.
- **"Standard taps only"** on every Watch board.
- **Purchase path:** "Upgrade" without a price on all List boards; chevrons on locked rows on A2-List; no 40-minute option.
- **Marks:** none reads as a timer; the ripple mark reads as sound; A against A1 "did not register".
- **Details:** red "End session" on B2-Session "looks like an error"; the empty email field on B3-Web was taken for a search box.

## 4. Keep / change

**Keep**
- B2-Session "Then…" line; A2-Session labelled ticks; done/next states on the B and B3 tiles.
- A2-Web and B2-Web headlines; B2-Web device line; A2-Web "No account and no server".
- A3-Session "left in this session"; the pill on A3-Watch and B-Watch.
- Padlocks on A-List and B-List.

**Change**
- A-Session, A1-Session, A3-Session, B1-Session: time and tap count under each marker; mark delivered nudges.
- A2-Watch: restore "1 tap" beside the circles.
- All Watch boards: remove or reword "Standard taps only"; consider dropping the "15 min" title.
- A2-List: padlocks instead of chevrons on locked rows.
- All List boards: show the one-time price at "Upgrade" and "Your own timers".
- B-Web, B-Brand: drop "Psst"; move the palette towards B3.
- B2-Session: restyle "End session".
- B3-Web: label the email field.

## 5. Best of the A family and best of the B family for this audience

- **Best of A: A2.** 5 for comprehension, for_me and fits_setting; ranked second. Faults: A2-List chevrons, A2-Watch circles.
- **Best of B: B2.** Ranked first; 5 for in_session_clarity and would_join_waitlist. Fault: red "End session".
- **A versus B: no family winner.** She "would not choose between them on looks". The product-led variants lead in both families. A leads on fits_setting, B2 on in-session clarity. The deciding elements, written tap counts and plain wording, can be applied to either family.

## 6. What I would not conclude from this

- One synthetic persona: scores and ranking are not measurements.
- Room visibility, glare and distance: she called her own statements "guesses".
- Haptics, motion (including the A3 rings) and wrist glanceability cannot be judged from stills. The taps and the Watch are what she would buy; neither was tested.
- Nothing on mark or typeface: neither registered, which may be an effect of static boards.
- Layout is confounded with palette in the stimulus; her transfer answers are imagined.
- Price sensitivity: no board shows a price.
- Whether this persona represents trainers rests on unverified desk research.
