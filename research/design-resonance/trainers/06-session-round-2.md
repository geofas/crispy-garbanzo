# Session round 2 — trainers (participant)

Synthetic persona (Helen, independent trainer and speaker). Not a real person. Everything below is a hypothesis to check with real trainers, not evidence.

## User Research Director's questions

1. **Next 40-minute talk.** Only if I have a 40-minute timer, which means I have paid. Then B2 or A2; I would not choose between them on looks. I start it on the phone as I am introduced and put the phone in my bag or face-down. The watch is on my wrist. I do not plan to look at either. I would look at the watch once after a discussion, if I had lost count of the taps. So the phone session screen matters for about ten seconds, at the start. The Watch screen and the taps are what I am buying, and the taps cannot be judged here.
2. **B as it stands.** I would use it, with the phone face-down or in my pocket. The colour stops me displaying it, not using it. It is a preference about being seen, not a barrier.
3. **What those statements rest on.** All I saw is that A3-List has dark text on a light ground, A-Session is dark with pale digits, and B-Session has a large purple block. "Easier in a bright room", "the room would see" and "a front row would see" are my guesses from that. I am unsure of all three: glare, distance and screen brightness are not on the boards.
4. **After installing.** I read the list. I start the 15 to feel the taps. If they are too faint while I am moving, I stop there. If they work, I tap "Your own timers". If I see a price and it is a one-off I would not have to think about, I pay and build 40 minutes with nudges at 10, 5 and 2. If I see no price, or a subscription, I close it and use the free 15 for exercises only. On A2-List I would tap "Workshop · 90 min" first; the arrow looks like it opens.

## Design Director's questions

**1. Lectern.** Most to least comfortable: A, B3, B2, B1, B. B2 and B1 I would put there; they are bright but plain. B needs a different colour, not a smaller block: B3 has the same block and is fine. A light session screen in A's colours would be acceptable. Dark is what I am used to, not a requirement. A guess, as above.

**2. What comes next.** Keep the "Then…" line (B2). I lose the done/next marking from the tiles and the sense of distance from the bar. It holds on a dark screen; it is a sentence. Tiles on A's dark ground would also be fine. A and B1 with time and tap count under each dot would be close to level. Still missing: which nudge has already gone on A. The written counts matter more than the format.

**3. Classroom app.** (c) the colours change it most; (a) the wording is second. (b) I would not notice by itself. (d) makes no difference to "classroom", though it removes the volume-symbol reading. "Psst" on dark A-Web: an advert being clever, no longer a primary teacher. Still not how I talk. A-Web to A2-Web: the words, mostly. A2-Web also shows the Watch, which makes it a description of a thing, not a slogan.

**4. Watch.** Keep: big digits, the "5 min left · 1 tap" line, End. Remove without missing: "15 min" at the top; "Standard taps only". A2-Watch: the problem is the missing words. With "1 tap" beside them the circles are fine; alone on the phone they would not work either. The doubt is the same on all five. Only the caption under B-Watch and B2-Watch reduces it, and that is not on the watch. If the line were absent I would miss nothing. None carries too much. A3-Watch and B-Watch put the nudge in a pill, which I find first. "End" is large on all of them; whether I would hit it by accident cannot be judged.
