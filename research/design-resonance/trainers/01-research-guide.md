# Moderator guide — trainers

Simulated session. No real users are involved; answers are hypotheses to check with real trainers.

## How to work through this

- Answer in writing, in your own words, as you would speak. Think aloud: write what you notice first, then what you make of it.
- There are no right answers. We are testing the designs, not you.
- If you cannot judge something from a still image (taps, motion, how it reads on a moving wrist), say "cannot judge" and why. Do not guess.
- Work through the variants in this order: A, A1, A2, A3, B, B1, B2, B3. Answer each variant on its own terms. Do not compare or rank until the closing section.
- Quote the words or point to the element you are reacting to.

## Warm-up

1. Describe the sessions you run: setting, length, group size, alone or with someone.
2. How do you keep track of time during a session today? What do you carry or rely on?
3. Tell me about the last session where timing did not go as planned. What happened, and what did you do afterwards?

## Task 1 — First impressions

Boards: `<V>-Brand.png`, `<V>-Web.png`. Per variant:

1. What is this? Describe it as you would to a colleague.
2. Who do you think it is made for? What tells you that?
3. Picture it on your phone or wrist in the place you work. How would it sit there?
4. What, if anything, is unclear or missing?

## Task 2 — In-session use

Boards: `<V>-Session.png`, `<V>-Watch.png`. Imagine you are mid-session, in front of your group. Per variant:

1. What can you read in one glance? List it in the order you saw it.
2. What did you have to search for, or could not find?
3. What would the people in the room see or notice, if anything?
4. What do you understand about what happens next, and when?
5. What do you understand about the tap patterns and what each means? Where does that understanding come from on the screen?
6. What can you not judge from a still image?

## Task 3 — Timer list and upgrade

Board: `<V>-List.png`. Per variant:

1. What do you see on this screen? What would you do first?
2. How do the timers shown relate to the sessions you described in the warm-up?
3. Some items appear unavailable. What do you think is going on, and what is your reaction?
4. What do you expect to happen if you press the upgrade control? What would you want to know before pressing it?

## Task 4 — Words

All boards. Per variant:

1. Copy out the headline and any lines that stood out, for any reason.
2. What does each say to you? Who does it sound like?
3. Which words would you use yourself when talking about timekeeping, and which would you not?
4. Is anything stated that you doubt or would want shown?

## Closing — only after all eight

1. Which variants stayed with you, and why?
2. Which would you be comfortable using in front of a group, which not, and what decides it?
3. What differences between variants mattered to you, and which did not register?
4. What would you need to see or try before forming a firm view?
